# Find

**Find** searches the firm's records from one box: cases, contacts, filings, exhibits, invoices and time.

- Open it from its program, or press **Ctrl+Shift+F** (Cmd+Shift+F on a Mac) anywhere.
- It searches as you type. Every word must appear; capitals don't matter, and part of a word is enough.
- Case numbers match with or without dashes.
- Results are grouped by type. Pick one to open it in its program.

To go to a program, a page or a form instead, press **Ctrl+K** (Cmd+K) or **/** and type its name.

See also: [The Case File](#help/cases).
