# Writing a filing

**Court Filings** holds the court forms, grouped by court, and the firm's own templates: petitions, motions, letters and orders for judges.

1. Pick a form from the list on the left (the ☰ button at phone width). Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) jumps straight to any form.
2. Choose who you're filing as in the top bar, then **Fill from character** to fill in your details.
3. Fill in each section. The section headings count what's filled, and required fields are marked.
4. Check the live preview beside the form. **Highlight unfilled** shows what's still empty.

Date boxes take a typed date, such as 29 Oct 1990, or open a calendar. A date that doesn't exist, such as 31 Feb, is outlined in red and isn't used: the box says which date is kept.

Drafts save as you type. **Undo** and **Redo** step through your changes, and **Draft history** brings back an earlier draft.

## The Form menu

- **Pick a case…** fills the form from one of the firm's cases.
- **Open case in Case File** goes to the form's case.
- **Law Library…** inserts a citation.
- **Filing history…** lists what you've filed, to open again as a new draft.

Under a text box, **Insert exhibit** adds a case exhibit, such as "Exhibit C (Dashcam footage)".

## Your own templates

**+ New template** at the top of the forms list opens the template builder.

1. Give the template a title, such as MOTION TO CONTINUE.
2. Add blocks: a **Heading**, a **Paragraph** of fixed wording, a **Field** to fill in (short text, long text, a date or a number), a **Repeating list** (numbered items, add as many as you need) or a **Signature** (date, name, bar number, firm and role).
   On a **Field**, **Fills from** fills it for you: from your character (name, title, bar number, phone, address, email), from the case you pick (case number, plaintiff, defendant, court, date filed) or with today's date. Tick **Required** and the copy buttons warn while it's empty.
3. **Up** and **Down** move a block, **Delete** removes it. The preview shows the filing as you go.
4. **Save template**. It lists under **My Templates** and opens like any form.

**Make my own version** on any form copies it into the builder, so you can start from a form you already use. Its wording comes in as paragraphs you can change, keeping the form's look. Everything else, like the court heading and tables, comes in as **Original text** blocks: you can move or delete them but not edit them. **Fields in the original text** lets you rename their fields. The form itself never changes.

**Edit** beside a template opens it in the builder again, where **Delete template** removes it. Templates sync to the firm and are in the backup.
