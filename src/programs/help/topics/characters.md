# Characters

A character holds the details a filing needs, such as name, title, phone and address. **Fill from character** copies them into a form.

- Add and edit characters in **Settings** (the **Control Panel** in the Classic theme), or with **File → Characters…** in any program.
- The top bar shows who you're **filing as**. Switch it there; it's the same in every program.
- Each character can have an hourly rate for the [Ledger](#help/ledger).
- A Court Commissioner also gets the [J.A.P.](#help/jap).

Characters are saved in this browser. To keep them safe or use them on another device, see [Sync and backups](#help/sync).
