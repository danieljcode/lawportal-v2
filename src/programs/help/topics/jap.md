# J.A.P.

The **J.A.P.** is the Judicial Advancement Portfolio for Court Commissioners I and II. Only a character who is a Court Commissioner sees it.

- Work through the task lists and watch your progress.
- Copy the finished portfolio as BBCode for the forum.

Each character keeps their own J.A.P., and it syncs with them.

See also: [Characters](#help/characters), [Sync and backups](#help/sync).
