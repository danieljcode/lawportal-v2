# Clients

**Clients** has three pages.

- **New client intake** opens a case from one form and prints a client sheet.
- **Conflict check** looks for a name on either side of every firm case, gives a clear verdict and a memo, and keeps a log.
- **Rolodex** keeps a card for every client, judge, officer, attorney, witness and business the firm deals with, with their phone, address and notes.

In a filing, party names have **Pick from Rolodex**, which fills in the name, address and phone.
