# Clients

**Clients** has three pages.

- **New client intake** opens a case from one form and prints a client sheet. See below.
- **Conflict check** looks for a name on either side of every firm case, gives a clear verdict and a memo, and keeps a log.
- **Rolodex** keeps a card for every client, judge, officer, attorney, witness and business the firm deals with, with their phone, address and notes.

## Taking on a client

The intake takes you from first contact to an opened case. What you type is kept on this device, even after a refresh, until the case opens or you press **Discard**.

- **Run conflict check** checks the client and the opposing party against the firm's cases, right on the intake. Change either name and the result is marked out of date. If there are possible matches, tick that you've reviewed them before opening the case; they don't stop you. **Memo and details** opens the full result on the Conflict check page.
- **The Rolodex** picks who the client is. A client with the same name is picked for you. If someone only might be them, you choose, so nobody is added twice.
- **Open case** opens the case and links the client to it. The next steps are then a click away: open the case, prepare an engagement letter with the client's details filled in, or open their retainer. They're optional, and nothing is sent or signed for you.

In a filing, party names have **Pick from Rolodex**, which fills in the name, address and phone.
