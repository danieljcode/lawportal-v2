# The home screen

The home screen has a tile for each program, and below them **Needs attention**: what to do next.

The **work list** puts everything that's due in one place, most urgent first:

- **Court**: deadlines and hearings on cases, from the case's **Deadlines and hearings**.
- **Action**: a case's **next actions** with a due date. Tick one off here and it's done on the case too.
- **Billing**: invoices that aren't paid in full, with what's owed. Record payments in the [Ledger](#help/ledger).

Overdue items come first, then the soonest. Court dates and actions show for the next two weeks; unpaid invoices always show. Dates are in-game.

**My work** shows the work of the character you're filing as: court dates on cases they work, actions given to them, and bills on their cases. **All firm** shows everyone's, with who each action is for. With no character chosen, it shows the whole firm.

Below the work list are your **recent filings**, to open again, and your **recent cases**.

## Quick add

The round **+** at the bottom right, on the home screen and on an open case, adds something to a case in a couple of taps, without opening the Case File or the Ledger. Pick **Note**, **Time** or **Next action**, check the case (the one you have open, else the one changed last), fill it in and press **Add**. Notes and next actions go on the case as the character you're playing; time is logged for them today, in game time, so choose a character first. Add as many as you like, then close it.
