# The Case File

The **Case File** holds every firm case, with its lead and the characters working it. Search and filter the list to find one.

Each case has:

- its **details**;
- **notes**: short dated entries, newest first, each with when it was added (in game time) and who added it. **+ Add note** adds one as the character you're playing; **Edit** changes one and **Delete** removes it after asking. A case's old single notes box is kept as its first note. [Find](#help/find) and conflict checks search every note;
- **next actions**: the everyday work, like _Call the client_, each with who's doing it (or Unassigned) and an optional due date. Overdue ones are marked. Tick one off and it moves to **Done**, where you can reopen it; **Edit** changes it;
- **billing**: the time logged, what isn't on an invoice yet, and what's been invoiced, paid and is still owed, from the [Ledger](#help/ledger). A client's retainer isn't counted, except what has paid an invoice on this case. **Log time**, **Invoices** and **Open in Ledger** open the Ledger for just this case, with a link back;
- **deadlines and hearings**;
- the **filings** made for it;
- **contacts** from the Rolodex, each in a role such as client or witness;
- its **evidence**: lettered exhibits (A, B, C…) with links and dates. Each exhibit has a **chain of custody** you can copy into a filing or print;
- a **timeline**: all of the above in date order, newest first and grouped by game day, to catch up on a case at a glance. **Show** picks one type, such as filings only. Click an entry to go to it; time and invoices open in the Ledger. A next action shows the day it was ticked off; ones ticked off before the timeline came in are under **No date**.

**Upcoming** lists the next 14 days, overdue first. The Case File shows a dot when something is due within 48 hours.

**Firm Record** adds up how closed cases ended, with the win rate, for the firm and each lawyer. When you set a case to **Closed**, pick its outcome (won, lost, settled, dismissed, plea deal or withdrawn), with a date and an optional note; you can add one to an already closed case the same way. Filter by matter type and dates. **Print / PDF** prints it and **Copy summary** copies a few lines of text. Win rate is wins out of the cases won or lost, and **Closed with no outcome yet** lists what still needs one.

**Court Docket** is for judges (a title such as Judge, Justice, Magistrate or Commissioner). It lists the cases before them, soonest next date first, each one **Pending**, **Scheduled**, **Under advisement** (the status is Awaiting judgement, so a ruling is owed) or **Closed**. Filter by those, and pick a judge template and **Start** to open it with the case's details filled in.
