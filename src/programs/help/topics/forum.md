# Copying to the forum

The buttons under the preview turn a finished filing into what you need.

- **Copy for forum** copies the filing as formatted text. Paste it into the forum editor.
- **Copy BBCode** copies it as BBCode, for an editor that only takes plain text.
- **Download .docx** saves it as a Word file.
- **Download PDF** opens your browser's print dialog with just the filing. Pick **Save as PDF**.
- The **Discord** tab splits the filing into messages that fit: 2,000 characters each, or 4,000 with Nitro.

Court forms never carry the firm's name or colours, so a filing looks official wherever you post it.

Always read a filing through before you post it.
