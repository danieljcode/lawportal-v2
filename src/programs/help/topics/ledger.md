# Ledger

The **Ledger** keeps the firm's time and money.

- **Time:** log the time each character spends on a case, by date and activity, with a note. Or start a timer on a case; **Stop and log** saves the time it ran. Filter by case, character and dates to see the hours and what they're worth.
- **Rates:** give each character an hourly rate in the Characters dialog, or set one rate for a whole case.
- **Invoices:** turn a case's time into a numbered invoice with the letterhead. Record payments as they come in, then print it, copy it for the forum or Discord, or download it. An invoice is due 30 days after it's issued.
- **Accounts receivable** lists what's still owed, oldest first.
- **Retainers:** record what a client deposits and pay their invoices from it. The Ledger warns when a balance runs low and writes the letter asking for more.
- **Reports:** billable hours, revenue by month and open cases, each with a chart and a CSV download.
