# Sending a filing

The buttons above the preview turn a finished filing into what you need.

- **Download .docx** saves it as a Word file.
- **Download PDF** opens your browser's print dialog with just the filing. Pick **Save as PDF**.
- The **Discord** tab splits the filing into messages that fit: 2,000 characters each, or 4,000 with Nitro. Copy each part in turn.

The preview shows the filing as it will look on the page. **Highlight unfilled** marks what's still empty.

Court forms never carry the firm's name or colours, so a filing looks official wherever you send it.

Always read a filing through before you send it.
